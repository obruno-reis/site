Brain Gym

Ginástica do Cérebro (Brain Gym) is a neuroeducation franchise that turns games into brain training — building focus, memory, and social skills through play, for everyone from young children to seniors. The identity had to reach very different people at once: kids and neurodivergent learners, their parents, and the psychologists and educators who run each unit. It needed to feel playful without being childish, and credible without turning clinical. So we built a warm, grounded identity that greets a six-year-old and a seventy-year-old with the same open hand.

// Category
Branding & Visual Identity
// Timeline
3 weeks

// Project Goals

Unify a multi-audience franchise under one identity that scales from a single unit to a national network. Balance play and rigor so the brand feels fun to children and trustworthy to the families and professionals who choose it.

// The Result

A complete brand guide — symbol, logo, color hierarchy, typography, and a full photographic direction — that keeps Ginástica do Cérebro consistent across storefronts, social, print, and franchise training. The system carries real neuroscience with a light, human touch, giving every unit a recognizable presence that feels as welcoming as its rooms.
