Clínica Mar Atlântico

Clínica Mar Atlântico is an online-first mental-health clinic offering psychiatry, psychology, and nutrition under one standard: clinical excellence delivered with real humanity — support for every phase of life. Mental-health care often forces a false choice between authority and warmth, a clinic feeling either rigorous or welcoming but rarely both. Mar Atlântico needed an identity that held the two together: the seriousness of expert medicine and the feeling of being genuinely heard, without judgment. So we anchored the brand in its own name — the Atlantic — using the ocean as a language for depth, calm, and steady support.

// Category
Branding & Web Design
// Timeline
2 weeks

// Project Goals

Build an identity that signals medical excellence and human warmth at once, so neither undercuts the other. Give an online-first clinic a presence patients trust before the first appointment — coherent from the landing page to the consultation.

// The Result

A complete identity that turns the Atlantic into a system. The symbol layers concentric arcs — ripples on water, and also the sense of being heard — around a central eye-and-leaf form, reading at once as a wave, a horizon, and a vessel. Set in deep Atlantic navy with a light-sky accent and a rounded, humanist wordmark, it carries authority and warmth in the same breath. The language scales from website and social to out-of-home, with portraits masked inside the brand's own wave shapes, giving a distributed online practice a calm, grounded presence patients recognize on sight.
